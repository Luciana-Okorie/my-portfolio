A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Curious by default.

I'm a Software Engineer and Full-Stack Developer who enjoys turning ideas into working software — especially when the problem is a little difficult.

My recent work has taken me from interfaces and APIs into event-driven architecture, real-time collaboration, distributed systems, AI integrations, databases, containers and observability.

I don't want to only know how to make an application work. I want to understand what happens when it gets busy, when a service fails, when data goes out of sync, and when users need it to feel effortless.
